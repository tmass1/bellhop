Symbol style, icon by icon

The app icon, and the symbol Bellhop would lift off it, at 2.5× so the edges are visible. Icons without a flat background tile fall back to a shaded silhouette (marked in amber).

Color (current)Easiest to recognize, but the only color in a monochrome bar, so keepers stand out more than the icons around them.
GrayscaleThe same tile without its color. Reads as an app at a glance and blends better, but the tile shape still makes it look heavier than a menu bar glyph.
SymbolClosest to a real menu bar icon: one color, follows light, dark and the selected state. Depends on the icon having a clear symbol on a flat tile; busy icons fall back.
SilhouetteThe earlier "Mono" option. Every tile becomes the same rounded blob, so it's shown only to rule it out.
