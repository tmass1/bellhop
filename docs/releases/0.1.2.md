Hover fixes, and keepers that open on hover.

- **Resting on Bellhop's icon opens the panel again.** On macOS 27 the icon could lose the hover a moment after the pointer arrived, so the panel never opened.
- **Keepers open on hover.** Rest on a keeper for a moment and its app opens, as a click does: its menu, or its panel.
- **Keepers' panels open to the left,** hanging from the keeper's right edge, so they open toward the middle of the screen instead of past its edge.
- **A clear message when an app ignores Bellhop.** Some apps, like CopyClip right after it launches, only open on a real click. Clicking their keeper or row now says so, and that one click on the real icon usually fixes it until the app restarts.
