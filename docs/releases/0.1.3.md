A steadier menu bar on screens without a notch.

- **No more flickering « arrow.** Right as an app came to the front, Bellhop could measure its menus before they were laid out and make the divider too wide, so macOS kept re-fitting the bar. Bellhop now waits for the menus and measures again.
- **Two displays measure correctly.** When the front app's menus are on your other display, Bellhop now sizes the divider for both bars instead of only the active one.
